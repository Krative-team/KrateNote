What is KrateNote?

KrateNote is a voice note app for students. Record lectures clearly even from the back bench with Class Mode, transcribe with Whisper, and translate notes to Pidgin, Ibibio, Efik, Yoruba, Hausa and 30 languages.

Why students use KrateNote

  • Record lecturer from far distance — Mic boost + noise filter for classroom
  • Auto-transcribe to text — Whisper transcription, no missing words
  • Translate notes instantly — Pidgin, Ibibio, Efik, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, French and more
  • Save, share and export to PDF — Organise by folder & subject

How KrateNote works

1. Login or create account  •  2. Choose folder & subject  •  3. Tap Class Mode  •  4. Tap START RECORDING  •  5. Tap SAVE. Your notes sync across devices.

Can KrateNote record lecturer from far distance?

Yes. Class Mode uses mic boost, noise suppression and Whisper transcription to capture lecturer voice clearly even when you sit at the back of the class.

Does KrateNote translate to Pidgin, Ibibio and Efik?

Yes. KrateNote translates notes to Nigerian Pidgin, Ibibio, Efik, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo and 25+ other languages.

Is KrateNote free for students?

Yes. Students can create an account, record lectures, transcribe and save notes for free.
